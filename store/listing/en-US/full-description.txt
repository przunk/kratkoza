Kratkoza is a pure, thoughtful Sudoku game designed for Android. No advertising, no paywalls, no artificial timers locking you out of the game — just clean logic, elegant design, and responsive controls.

CLASSIC 9×9 SUDOKU
• Adheres strictly to the standard rules of pencil-and-paper Sudoku.
• Every generated board has exactly one unique solution and requires no guessing.
• Initial givens are clearly distinguished from your entered digits.

5 BALANCED DIFFICULTY LEVELS
Difficulty is graded by the human logical techniques required to solve each board, not just by how many cells are empty:
• Easy — singles only, perfect for relaxing or learning.
• Normal — singles, requiring thorough scanning across the grid.
• Hard — locked candidates (pointing and claiming).
• Expert — naked and hidden pairs.
• Master — triples, X-Wing, XY-Wing, and Swordfish.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Smart Candidate Notes: Enter pencil marks with auto-removal when a digit is placed. Clear all notes with a single long press.
• Error Detection: Immediate conflict highlighting without a punitive lives system.
• Full Undo: Unlimited, step-by-step history covering digits and notes.
• Erase & Remaining Counts: Clear cells easily and optionally see how many of each digit remain.
• Pause & Auto-Save: Step away anytime. Your game state and timer are automatically preserved.

DAILY SUDOKU & LEADERBOARDS
• A fresh, deterministic Daily challenge every day across 3 difficulties (Easy, Hard, Expert).
• Optional Google Play Games sign-in for competitive public leaderboards.
• Past Daily puzzles remain playable offline as unranked games.

STATISTICS & PROGRESS
• Detailed local records: games started, completed, completion rate, best times, and average times per difficulty.
• Player XP and leveling system that rewards completed boards.
• Board Archive: Revisit and replay previously solved boards.
• Share boards with friends using lightweight puzzle codes.

DESIGN & ACCESSIBILITY
• Calm, high-readability interface with distinct 3×3 block boundaries.
• Dark, Light, and System themes.
• Four carefully chosen color palettes (Slate, Classic, Forest, Dusk) plus a dedicated High Contrast mode.
• Full bilingual support: English and Polish, switchable in-app.
• TalkBack accessibility labels on every cell.

100% OFFLINE & PRIVACY-FIRST
Kratkoza works entirely without an internet connection. No account is required. No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs are included. Your puzzles, history, and settings remain on your device.
