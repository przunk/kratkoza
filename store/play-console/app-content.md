## App access

Select **All functionality is available without special access**.
There are no paywalls, subscriptions, mandatory login credentials, or geographic restrictions.

Reviewer notes:
> Kratkoza requires no credentials or special access to review. All Sudoku puzzles, candidate notes,
> themes, and offline Daily puzzles are directly accessible from the main menu. Signing in to
> Google Play Games for leaderboards is optional and uses the standard system account chooser.

## Target audience and content

- **Target age groups:** **13–15, 16–17, and 18 and over**.

- **Appeal to children:** No (the app is not specifically designed to attract children).
